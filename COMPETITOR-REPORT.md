# Competitive Intelligence Report: Digital Edge Studio
**URL:** https://digitaledgestudio.com
**Date:** 2026-04-12 (updated from 2026-03-12 baseline)
**Competitors Analyzed:** 10
**Competitive Position: Moderate-Strong — unique niche positioning, growing content footprint, authority still the bottleneck**

---

## Executive Summary

One month after the initial competitor report, Digital Edge Studio's competitive landscape is clearer. The Wollongong web design market has **10-12 active agencies** plus several national tradie-specialist players. Digital Edge has made significant progress — 116 indexed pages, 25 blog posts, 7 comparison pages, and a comprehensive location page strategy — but still lacks the domain authority and Google reviews needed to crack page 1 for primary keywords.

**What's changed since March:**
- Digital Edge now has 27 location pages, 12 industry pages, and 7 vs/ comparison pages (up from 3 location pages and 2 industry pages)
- MadWeb's pricing is now confirmed: **$599 setup + $99/mo subscription** — undercutting Digital Edge's $1,200 one-time starter price
- Love My Online Marketing has added **AI Search Optimisation** as a service — previously only Digital Edge offered AEO/GEO in the Wollongong market
- New competitors identified: **Zammeth**, **Canty Digital** ($99 single-page sites), **WP Creative** (1,200+ projects)

**Biggest competitive advantage:** Digital Edge remains the **only Wollongong agency combining tradie specialisation + local focus + transparent pricing + AI search (AEO/GEO) services**. No other local competitor offers all four.

**Biggest competitive threat:** MadWeb's subscription model ($599 + $99/mo) is an easier entry point than $1,200 upfront. Love My Online Marketing adding AI search optimisation erodes one of Digital Edge's differentiators.

**Top 3 strategic recommendations:**
1. Build Google reviews to 10+ (every competitor has years of reviews; you have none visible)
2. Create measurable case studies with % results (MyTradieSite's format remains the gold standard)
3. Consider adding a low-barrier subscription option to counter MadWeb's pricing model

## Detailed Competitor Profiles

### 1. MadWeb (madweb.com.au) — CLOSEST COMPETITOR

**Why they matter:** Most similar positioning — affordable, Wollongong-based, lead-driven. And they've now revealed aggressive pricing that undercuts Digital Edge.

**Messaging:**
- Headline: "Wollongong Web Design Experts"
- Value prop: High-quality custom websites, affordable, fast turnaround, zero fluff
- Tone: Casual, direct, no-nonsense — very similar to Digital Edge

**Pricing (CONFIRMED):**
- Professional websites from **$599 setup + $99/month**
- Unlimited updates included in monthly plan
- Single-page sites available (targeting sole traders/tradies)
- eCommerce: custom pricing
- Free annual redesign mentioned

**Services:** Web design, SEO, custom builds, subscription support plans, single-page sites

**Social Proof:** Client testimonials on site. Claims "real results" but no specific metrics.

**Technology:** Built on **Wix** (confirmed via Wix Marketplace listing) — a vulnerability given they sell "custom websites."

**SEO Strategy:** Multiple location pages (Wollongong, Wodonga, etc.), service-specific pages, active blog. Aggressive local SEO.

**SWOT:**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Strengths** | Subscription pricing (lower barrier), strong local SEO, active content, multiple location pages |
| **Weaknesses** | Built on Wix (slow, template-limited), no tradie focus, no measurable case studies |
| **Opportunities for DE** | Highlight custom-coded Next.js sites vs Wix. Emphasise performance scores. Show page speed comparisons |
| **Threats** | $599 entry price undercuts $1,200. Similar target market. Growing SEO presence |

**Pricing Comparison Impact:**
| | Digital Edge | MadWeb |
|---|---|---|
| Entry price | $1,200 one-time | $599 + $99/mo |
| Year 1 total cost | $1,200 + $99/mo = $2,388 | $599 + $99×12 = $1,787 |
| Year 2 total cost | $99/mo = $1,188 | $99/mo = $1,188 |
| 3-year total | $4,776 | $4,163 |
| Ownership | Client owns the site | Unclear (Wix subscription dependency) |

MadWeb is **$601 cheaper in year 1** but locks clients into a Wix platform dependency. Digital Edge should counter by emphasising **ownership** and **platform independence**.

### 3. Love My Online Marketing (lovemyonlinemarketing.com) — ASPIRATIONAL

**Why they matter:** The most established Wollongong web agency. Now directly competing on AI search optimisation.

**Messaging:**
- H1: "Web Design & Conversion Agency in Wollongong"
- Value prop: "Bringing Your Vision To Life" — award-winning, AI-ready websites
- Tone: Professional but approachable

**NEW SERVICE (since March):** AI Search Optimisation (AEO & GEO)
- "Be found in AI-powered answers from tools like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews"
- This was previously a unique differentiator for Digital Edge
- **AEO/GEO page deep dive (April 2026):** ~1,500 words of generic copy. 10 service bullets that overlap heavily. 12 FAQ items (most collapsed). 5-step process (bloated). Zero statistics, zero research citations, zero concrete examples. Requires clients to rebuild sites on their hosting platform before providing AEO/GEO — a lock-in tactic. Social proof on the page is all web design testimonials, none mentioning AI search results. No industry targeting (completely generic). No pricing transparency ("Request a Callback" only). No technical specifics (no mention of schema types, llms.txt, or how AI actually selects answers).

**Services:** Web design, logo/branding, SEO, eCommerce, Google Ads, AI Search Optimisation (NEW), consulting, HubSpot CRM

**Social Proof:** 
- 10+ years in business
- "Three Best Rated Best Business of 2025" badge
- 5-star reviews (claimed)
- Clients from small startups to national corporations
- Physical Wollongong location

**SWOT:**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Strengths** | 10+ years, awards, broadest service offering, AI search now offered, HubSpot expertise |
| **Weaknesses** | No tradie specialisation, likely premium pricing, no transparent pricing, generic positioning, AEO/GEO page is fluffy with no technical depth, forces site rebuild lock-in, no sourced stats |
| **Opportunities for DE** | Be the tradie/SMB specialist vs their generalist approach. AEO/GEO page now significantly deeper (13 FAQs vs 12, sourced stats, competitive positioning section, no lock-in messaging). Out-content them on AI search topics with blog posts and technical specifics |
| **Threats** | Adding AI search service creates perception of parity even though their offering is shallower. Strong authority and longevity |

**Key Action:** Love My Online Marketing adding AI search means Digital Edge must **deepen** its AEO/GEO content — not just offer the service, but become the authority on the topic (blog posts, case studies, free tools, guides). Being first with a service page isn't enough; owning the educational content around it is what builds authority.
